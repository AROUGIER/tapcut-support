# Support

**My taps land slightly off.** Sound takes time to reach you, especially over Bluetooth.
Settings › Calibrate This Output: ten seconds of clicks, once per headset or pair of earbuds.

**The passage is too fast to tap precisely.** In the rhythm studio, slow down to 0.75× or 0.5×: the
cuts stay locked to the song.

**My Apple Music or Spotify song doesn't show up, or the video is silent.** These services don't
allow their songs in an exported video. Export without the music, then add the same song in
Instagram: Tap Cut tells you where to start it.

**I can't see my shared albums.** They need full access to Photos: Settings › Tap Cut › Photos ›
Full Access.

**The Places tab doesn't show names.** They come from Apple's maps service and need a connection;
they fill in on your next visit.

**Does deleting a reel touch my photos?** No. Only the copies Tap Cut made are erased.

Another question? [Write to us](https://github.com/AROUGIER/tapcut-support/issues).
